Evaluating a wallet-load payment model

At 360 World, a B2B wallet and settlement platform, I analyzed transaction-level data to compare processing fees on individual transactions with fees on wallet loads. Finance used the analysis to revise unit economics and merchant terms.

Adopted by Finance The analysis became the basis for revised unit economics and merchant commercial terms.

Problem

A per-transaction Stripe routing model shaped processing costs and merchant economics. The question was whether a different routing structure could materially improve them.

Approach

Compare the existing Stripe routing model with a wallet-load model at the same transaction demand. The alternative changes when a processing fee is incurred, so the analysis had to connect transaction behavior with the proposed commercial terms.

My contribution

I analyzed transaction-level data, modeled the alternative and presented the business case to Finance. I translated the processing-cost comparison into unit economics that could inform merchant terms.

Outcome

The model indicated roughly 50% lower processing costs, and Finance adopted the analysis for revised unit economics and merchant terms. This was a modeled opportunity; the case study does not establish realized savings.

How the comparison works
