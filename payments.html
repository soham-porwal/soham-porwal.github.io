360 World Associate PM, Payments Sep 2024–Mar 2025 Business analysis

A different route through payment economics

At 360 World, a B2B wallet and settlement platform, I analyzed transaction-level data and made the business case for a wallet-load alternative. The platform processed less than $10M annually across 10+ merchants in the period described.

Problem

A per-transaction Stripe routing model shaped processing costs and merchant economics. The question was whether a different routing structure could materially improve them.

Decision

Compare the existing flow with a wallet-load model and explain the commercial implications to Finance, rather than treating processing fees as a fixed input.

Implementation & ownership

I analyzed transaction-level data, modeled the alternative, and translated the result into a business case. The work connected product architecture with the unit economics used in merchant terms.

Outcome

Finance adopted the analysis as a basis for revised unit economics and merchant commercial terms. The roughly 50% estimate comes from the routing model; realized savings were not measured in this case study.
